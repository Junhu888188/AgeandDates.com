Business Days Between Two Dates

Use this working days calculator to count Monday through Friday between two dates.

Only Saturdays and Sundays are excluded. Public holidays are not deducted.

Frequently asked questions

How are business days calculated?

Business days are Monday through Friday. Only Saturdays and Sundays are excluded; public holidays are not deducted. Include or exclude either endpoint using the checkboxes.

How many business days are in a year?

It depends on the year. In 2026 there are 261 Monday-to-Friday business days and 104 weekend days, counting January 1 through December 31. Public holidays are not deducted.

What happens if the start and end dates are the same?

With both endpoints included, a weekday counts as one business day and a weekend date counts as one weekend day. If either endpoint is excluded, the count is zero.
