Calculate the duration and hours between two dates and times using your local wall-clock entries.

Uses local wall-clock time; daylight-saving changes are not applied

Frequently asked questions

How many hours are between two dates?

Enter the start and end dates and times to find the total hours. For example, January 1 at 09:00 to January 2 at 15:30 is 30.50 hours, or 1 day 6 hours 30 minutes.

Does this calculator account for daylight-saving time?

No. Uses local wall-clock time; daylight-saving changes are not applied. Time zones and clock changes are not used, so this may differ from actual elapsed time.

What if the start and end dates and times are the same?

Identical dates and times give 0 days 0 hours 0 minutes, 0.00 total hours, and 0 total minutes. An end date and time earlier than the start produces an error.
