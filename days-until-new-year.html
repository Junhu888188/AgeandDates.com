How Many Days Until New Year's Day?

We count one day for each midnight between today's date and January 1 in your local time zone.

Frequently asked questions

How many days until New Year 2027?

New Year 2027 falls on Friday, January 1. From December 31, 2026, it is one day away.

What does the countdown show on January 1?

It shows 0 days all day. At midnight on January 2, it starts counting down to January 1 of the following year.

Does daylight saving time change the count?

No. A calendar day counts as one day even when the clocks move forward or back.
